5. Arithmetic Proof
-----------------
- A jump occurs if the comparison fails.
- Comparison failed = Jump to 0x45ca (+442).
- Comparison passed = Continue to 0x454a (+314).
- The CPU was observed at +314.
- 314 < 442. 
- NO abstraction: The CPU pointer was literally 0xffffffff8b3fc57a (Base + 314).

Numerical Conclusion:
The instruction stream flowed precisely through the AT_FDCWD block because the CPU arrived at the address 0x454a, which is exclusively part of that branch's implementation.
